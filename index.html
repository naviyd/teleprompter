Eyeline Prompter

Paste your script, then open the camera. The words scroll in a band right under your front camera, so your eyes stay near the lens while you read.

  • Hold or mount the phone at arm's length or further. The further away the camera is, the less your eye movement shows.
  • Keep the prompter height low and the column narrow so your eyes barely move.
  • Tap the text to pause. Swipe it to jump back or ahead.
  • The saved video shows only you. The script never appears in the recording.
  • The label next to the size buttons shows the camera's real resolution. Face a window or a lamp: in dim light phones lower the frame rate and the picture gets soft.
  • On a laptop, the script sits in a narrow strip right under the webcam. Keyboard: Space to play or pause, R to record, arrow keys for speed, F for full screen.
  • Turn the phone sideways for landscape. The script moves into a column beside the camera, with the reading line level with the lens. Pick the orientation before you hit record, and turn off rotation lock.
